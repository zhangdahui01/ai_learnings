# A monk knocks the wooden fish and gains merit

## Given
- a monk who has entered the hall with merit 0

## When
- the monk knocks the wooden fish 3 times

## Then
- the merit shows 3 at once
- the merit still shows 3 after the hall has been reloaded

## Cleanup
- the monk leaves the temple
