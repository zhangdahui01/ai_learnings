# A monk who loses composure is shown out of the temple

## Given
- a monk seated in a hall where a stray dog is about to come in

## When
- the dog arrives and the monk does nothing

## Then
- the game ends with "定力不足"
- the monk is no longer in the hall

## When
- the visitor goes back to the gate

## Then
- the temple gate is shown again

## Cleanup
- no side effect: the monk has already left the hall
